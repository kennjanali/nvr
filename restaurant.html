Negrense soul, harvested steps away.

Signature Negrense dishes where every course is served with quiet, attentive care.

At Nature’s Village, every meal begins with heartfelt hospitality. We welcome you with fresh garden flavors, generous plates, and thoughtful service designed to make every moment feel warm, personal, and unhurried.

Much of what reaches your table was grown a few steps away at our own organic farm, handpicked by our chef.

Every dish is a small act of care: for the guest at the table, for the neighbours and artisans who help us, and for the land that feeds us all. Come as a guest — leave having tasted a gentler way of living.

Craft in every corner of the room

A striking chandelier, handcrafted from small wine bottles by a Negrense artist, takes center stage overhead. Complemented by textiles woven by local makers, the restaurant celebrates the beauty of local craftsmanship.

Dining here feels less like a hotel restaurant and more like being welcomed into a Negrense home that happens to have exceptional taste.

Grown here. Handpicked by our chefs.

Almost everything on your plate begins a few steps away, in our own organic farm. Chefs walk the rows each morning, choosing whatever is ripest — freshness isn't an aspiration here, it's simply how we cook.

Every ingredient choice carries a little more weight than usual: we grow what we can, and source the rest from neighbouring Negrense farmers. It keeps our food honest, our footprint light, and our table connected to the land.

Cold-pressed, blended, and poured to order — every shake and organic drink carries a little of our garden.
