Where every stay feels like coming home.

Here, you don't just see green—you walk through it, barefoot if you wish. Touch wood and stone polished by time, not trend. Listen to birdsong instead of buzz. Savor farm-to-table meals grown steps away.

Every decision here — composting in our gardens, eliminating single-use plastics, encouraging mindful choices like linen reuse — is a gesture of respect for the land, our people, and you, our guest.
